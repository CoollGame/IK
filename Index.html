Specialized Services (SRS, Light & Audio)

Premium Bi-LED Headlight Retrofit
Complete headlight overhaul and custom conversion. Installation of high-performance Bi-LED projectors/lenses, professional housing disassembly, and precise cutoff line adjustment for unmatched night visibility without blinding oncoming traffic.
SRS Airbag Module Reset (Crash Data Clear)
Advanced bench-flashing and software reset of your vehicle's Airbag (SRS) module after an accident. Hard codes and "Crash Data" completely cleared, restoring the original module back to factory working condition.
Airbag & Seatbelt Pretensioner Replacement
Safe, professional replacement of deployed airbags (steering wheel, passenger dash, side curtains) and locked or blown seatbelt pretensioners using OEM replacement units.
Premium Audio Retrofits & Upgrades
Upgrade your factory speakers to high-performance aftermarket units. I also specialize in high-trim conversions: transferring premium sound systems (like Titanium or Platinum audio packs) from donor vehicles into lower-spec models.
FORScan Key Programming
Programming and pairing new transponder keys/fobs using dealer-level FORScan software for supported models (Ford/Lincoln/Mazda). Essential service if you only have one working key and need a reliable duplicate.

Mechanical Repairs & Body Work

Deep computer scanning of vehicle modules. Complete reading, decoding, and clearing of trouble codes (CEL, ABS, SRS), live data monitoring, and custom module configuration.
Suspension & Steering Service
On-site replacement of wear-and-tear suspension components: shocks, struts, ball joints, control arms, and stabilizer (sway bar) links to restore ride quality and safety.
Brake Job Service
Full service replacement of brake pads, rotors, and caliper servicing. Complete inspection of lines and hardware performed right inside your garage.
